Privacy Policy

Uppercut Arena · Android app · Last updated 20 November 2026

In short: Uppercut Arena is an offline boxing game. Nothing leaves the device.

Information we collect

None. Uppercut Arena has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Fight progress, coins, unlocked gloves and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

Permissions

  • The only permission is vibration, used for haptic feedback during fights. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send any data off the device.

Children's privacy

Uppercut Arena is suitable for all ages. It does not collect, use or disclose personal information from anyone, including children, in line with Google Play's Families policy and COPPA.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
